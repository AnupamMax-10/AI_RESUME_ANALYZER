Slide 05 / 12
05. Deep Dive

Natural Language Processing (NLP) Pipeline

How unstructured resume prose is converted into structured candidate attributes

🔍 Information Extraction Mechanism
  • Entity Extraction: SpaCy's Statistical Entity Recognizer maps tokens to categories (PERSON, ORG, GPE).
  • Noun Chunking: Syntactic dependency parsing groups base noun phrases (e.g. "Convolutional Neural Networks") rather than single words.
  • Rule-Based Pattern Matcher: Utilizes spacy.matcher.Matcher to capture variations in phone numbers and email formats using regex.
  • Skills Dictionary Matching: Extracted tokens are cross-referenced with a comprehensive curated skills dictionary (skills.csv).
⚡ Extraction Flowchart
Raw PDF File
  ↓ [pdfminer3 TextConverter]
Normalized Raw Text Stream
  ↓ [SpaCy Tokenizer & Stopword Filter]
Linguistic Tokens & Noun Chunks
  ↓ [Matcher & Skills Lexicon Lookup]
Structured JSON: {Name, Email, Skills, Degree}

06. Core Algorithm

Predictive ATS Scoring Model

Objective 100-point heuristic formula based on recruiter screening benchmarks

📊 9-Section Recruiter Rubric
Resume SectionPointsRecruiter Impact
Projects Portfolio+19 ptsProof of execution & hands-on knowledge
Work Experience+16 ptsDemonstrates practical industry contributions
Achievements & Honors+13 ptsCompetitive differentiator from candidate pool
Education Credentials+12 ptsConfirms formal academic qualification
Certifications+12 ptsSignals specialized domain excellence
Interests & Hobbies+9 ptsTeam fit and cultural balance
Technical Skills+7 ptsKey for ATS scanner keyword hits
Objective / Summary+6 ptsClarifies immediate career direction
Internships+6 ptsReal-world team project involvement
🎯 Score Classification Tiers
75 – 100 : High Impact (ATS Ready)
Resume contains rich project proof, work experience, credentials, and skills. Strong likelihood of passing recruiter screen.
50 – 74 : Moderate (Needs Polish)
Covers basics but lacks certifications, quantifiable achievements, or detailed project portfolios.
0 – 49 : High Risk (Incomplete)
Missing multiple critical sections. High risk of automated rejection by enterprise ATS algorithms.
